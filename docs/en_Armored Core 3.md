# Armored Core 3

## What does randomization do to this game?

Mission unlocks, AC parts, and Arena unlocks are shuffled into the multiworld
item pool. You'll start with a limited garage and unlock missions, parts,
and funds as you and other players find your items.

## What is the goal?

Depending on your settings, either clear the final story mission, reach
a target Arena rank or both.
